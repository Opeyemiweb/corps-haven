Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Corps Haven ("we," "us") is operated by Bello Opeyemi, an independent individual operator based in Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. This policy explains what information we collect through corps-haven.netlify.app, how we use it, and the rights you have over it — in line with the principles of Nigeria's Data Protection Act and the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

1. What we collect

Corps Haven does not require an account to browse. We collect information only when you actively submit it:

  • Housing submissions: title, description, area, price, house type, your phone number, and an optional photo.
  • Placement submissions: company name, industry, area, role details, and a phone number and/or email.
  • Reports: the listing you're reporting and the reason you give.
  • Saved listings: stored only in your own browser (localStorage) — we never see or receive this list.

We do not currently ask for or store government ID numbers, NYSC call-up numbers, or any payment information.

2. How we use it

  • To display approved listings publicly, exactly as submitted.
  • To let an admin review a submission before it goes live.
  • To contact you if there's a problem with your submission or a report concerning it.
  • To calculate basic, non-identifying site statistics (e.g. total listings).

3. What's shown publicly

If your housing or placement listing is approved, the phone number and/or email you provided will be visible to anyone who chooses to reveal it on that listing. Do not submit a contact method you aren't comfortable with the public seeing. We do not currently offer an intermediated messaging system that hides your number entirely.

4. How long we keep it

Approved listings remain visible until they expire, are deleted by an admin, or you ask us to remove them. Rejected or removed listings are retained internally for a limited period for moderation records, then may be permanently deleted.

5. Your rights

In line with NDPC principles, you have the right to:

  • Ask what information we hold about a listing you submitted.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information.
  • Ask us to delete your listing and associated contact information.
  • Object to how your information is being used.

To exercise any of these, email danielopeyemi1954@gmail.com with details of the listing in question. We'll respond as promptly as we reasonably can.

6. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell your information. Data is stored with Supabase (our database/hosting provider) and the site itself is hosted via Netlify — both process data on our behalf under their own security practices, not as independent recipients of your data.

7. Security

We use industry-standard access controls (row-level security) to restrict who can approve, reject, or delete listings. No system is perfectly secure, and we can't guarantee absolute security of information you submit.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes meaningfully, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
