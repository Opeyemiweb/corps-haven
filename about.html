About Corps Haven

Corps Haven is a community listings board built to help NYSC corps members find housing and placement (PPA) opportunities near where they're posted — and to help outgoing corps members pass on what they know to the next batch.

Not affiliated with NYSC

Corps Haven is an independent platform and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official service of the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC). For official NYSC matters, always use the official NYSC portal at nysc.gov.ng.

How listings work today

Anyone can submit a housing listing or placement opportunity. Before anything goes public, it's reviewed by an admin to check it looks genuine and complete. That review currently means a human reads the submission for obvious red flags — it does not currently include a physical property inspection, ownership verification, or company registration checks. See our Terms & Verification Policy for the full, honest breakdown of what "reviewed" means right now.
