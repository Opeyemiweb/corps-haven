3. Verification Policy — what "reviewed" actually means today

We believe in being precise here rather than vague. When a listing is approved, here is exactly what has and hasn't happened:

CheckCurrently done?
An admin reads the submission for completeness and obvious red flagsYes
Phone number format is checkedYes
Physical inspection of the propertyNot yet
Confirmation the poster owns/manages the propertyNot yet
ID or NYSC call-up verification of the posterNot yet

In short: "approved" means it passed a basic human review, not that it has been independently verified as genuine. We're building toward stronger verification over time, and this page will be updated honestly as that changes.

4. Safety Policy

Never send money — rent, caution fees, deposits, or otherwise — before physically inspecting a property or confirming an offer directly and independently. Corps Haven has no way to guarantee any listing is legitimate.
  • Always meet in safe, public conditions when inspecting a property or meeting a poster for the first time.
  • Verify a company's legitimacy independently before relying on a listed placement.
  • Use the Report button on any listing that looks fake, expired, or suspicious — reports go directly to admin review.
